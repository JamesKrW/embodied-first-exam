## Task
Clean and jerk the barbell: lift it from the platform to your shoulders, then to locked arms overhead, and hold it there motionless until the down signal. (Embodied Olympics `clean-and-jerk`; the robot as a service: its head cameras, joint sensors and IMUs, and the venue's fixed camera only.)

## Difficulty
The bar is light but lies low, 0.04 m above the platform, and the hands are two-finger grippers that hold it by friction, near their tips at first. Turning it over from the hang to the shoulders, slowly, asks about half of what the elbow and shoulder motors give; too fast, and the bar slides out of the jaws. Nothing balances the robot under a load, and no built-in action bends down to the bar or straightens the knees. In standard mode the agent has no simulator and no state: it sees through the robot's cameras, acts through the client, and has 50 resets (only the last attempt is graded).

## Verification
The sim sidecar's referee judges the live attempt, and that is the verdict (`final.json`: `graded_by` live, `replays` 0;
no grading replay, owner 2026-10-17); the frozen start is checked on the live simulator (`instance_mismatch`:
infrastructure), and the video is rendered from the recorded attempt afterwards. `/tests/grade.py` reads
`/data/final.json`: pass iff the live attempt succeeded. The
rules: The barbell is an aluminium technique bar (5.0 kg, 1.835 m long; the grip section between the inner collars is 1.31 m long and 0.025 m thick; the sleeves are 0.2325 m long and 0.05 m thick and revolve on the bar), without discs or collars: 5.0 kg. It lies on the competition platform (4.0 m by 4.0 m, 0.1 m high), on which you stand. Your hands are two-finger grippers, open at the start; a hand holds the bar while the bar's grip section touches both of that gripper's jaws (a break of up to 0.05 s is not letting go); they hold it by the friction of their jaws only, so it can slip, turn in the jaws and drop. Lift-off: the bar may leave the platform only in both hands: you fail the moment the bar's axis is more than 0.03 m above where it lies at rest while fewer than two hands grip it. The attempt: once the bar's centre has been 0.48 m above the platform (knee height) the lift must be finished: from then on you fail if either hand lets go or if any part of the barbell touches the platform before the down signal. The clean: the bar must first rest at your shoulders: its axis within 0.15 m of both shoulder joints, both hands on it, both knees within 10.0 degrees of straight, both feet on the platform and on one line, the bar and your pelvis slower than 0.1 m/s, and you under the bar (your trunk upright: the cosine of its tilt at least 0.94; the bar's centre no more than 0.25 m, horizontally, from the point midway between your shoulder joints; the bar, seen from above, within 20.0 degrees of the line through them), with the attempt begun (the bar has been above knee height and touches nothing but you), all of it without a break for 0.5 s. You fail if the bar's axis is more than 0.25 m above the centre of your head before that (a lift from the platform to overhead in one movement is a snatch). The jerk and the finish: after the clean, bring the bar overhead. The final position is: the bar's axis at least 0.25 m above the centre of your head, both hands on it, both elbows within 10.0 degrees of straight (the angle between the upper arm's and the forearm's long axes), both knees within 10.0 degrees of straight (the angle hip, knee, ankle), both feet on the platform and on one line (their centres no more than 0.05 m apart along the horizontal direction square to the bar; under a bar tilted more than 60.0 degrees from level the feet never count as on one line), the bar and your pelvis slower than 0.1 m/s, and you under the bar as in the clean (trunk upright, the bar's centre over the middle of your shoulders, the bar along your shoulder line). Once it is held for 2.0 s without a break the down signal is given: that is success, and the attempt ends. At any time you fail if a part of you other than your feet touches the platform, if any part of you or of the barbell touches the floor beside the platform, or after 60 s. There is no other fall rule: you may squat as low as you like, and the bar may touch your body.
